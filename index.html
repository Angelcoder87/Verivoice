How to use

Keep the message exactly as received. Choose Voice, Text, or Screenshot, then tap “Check this information.”

Works best in Chrome. Your browser’s speech recognition turns your voice into text in this browser.

    VeriVoice is an assistant, never an oracle. Verdicts are evidence summaries with confidence levels — when evidence is thin, it says so.
