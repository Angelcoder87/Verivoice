# VeriVoice — Demo Video Script (~2.5 min)

**Tone:** calm, clear, Kenyan-context. Narration over screen recording of the live app.

| # | Time | Visual | Narration |
|---|------|--------|-----------|
| 1 | 0:00–0:20 | Title card: VeriVoice logo, "Verify. Understand. Act." | "In Kenya, the information we act on arrives as voice notes and forwarded screenshots — in Sheng, Kiswahili, and English. But how do you check it before you share it?" |
| 2 | 0:20–0:40 | Problem montage: WhatsApp-style voice note, forwarded screenshot | "A rumour about school fees. A fake tender. A distorted budget quote. Fact-checking sites are text-first and English-first. Verification has been a privilege of the well-informed." |
| 3 | 0:40–0:55 | App home screen, three input options highlighted | "VeriVoice meets people where they are. Send it exactly what you received: a voice note, text, or a screenshot." |
| 4 | 0:55–1:20 | Screen recording: upload a Sheng voice note → transcription appears → claims extracted | "I'll use a real scenario. This voice note, in Sheng, claims county schools have introduced a new fee starting next term. VeriVoice transcribes it, then extracts the claims." |
| 5 | 1:20–1:50 | Verdict cards appear one by one: Supported / Disputed / Unverified; sources listed; confidence shown | "Each claim gets a verdict with sources and a confidence level. The fee claim: disputed by the county's own circular. And look at the last card — 'unverified'. VeriVoice doesn't pretend to know. It says what the evidence supports and what it doesn't." |
| 6 | 1:50–2:10 | Language toggle EN↔SW; next-steps section; share-back card | "Results come in English or Kiswahili. Every answer ends with next steps: confirm with the county education office before paying anything. And a short summary card you can share straight back to the group chat." |
| 7 | 2:10–2:30 | Closing cards: pipeline diagram, then roadmap bullets | "Voice-first. Low-bandwidth. No account needed. Built for Kenya, designed for Africa. Next: verify by simply forwarding to a VeriVoice WhatsApp number." |
| 8 | 2:30–2:40 | End card: VeriVoice, "Receive → Understand → Verify → Explain → Share", repo + live demo URL | "VeriVoice. Information you can trust — and act on." |

**Recording checklist:**
- [ ] Record at 1080p, full-screen browser, hide bookmarks bar
- [ ] Prepare 3 test inputs beforehand: 1 voice note (Sheng school-fee rumour), 1 text claim (county budget quote), 1 screenshot (fake tender notice)
- [ ] Slow, deliberate cursor movement; pause 2s after each verdict appears
- [ ] TTS or owner-narrated audio; background music optional at -20dB
