**Recording checklist:**
- [ ] Record at 1080p, full-screen browser, hide bookmarks bar
- [ ] Prepare 4 test inputs beforehand: 1 voice note (Sheng school-fee rumour), 1 text claim (county budget quote), 1 screenshot (fake tender notice), 1 unrelated claim for the UNVERIFIED honesty moment (e.g. 'The registration fee for the conference is 5000, pay via M-PESA to the organizer.')
- [ ] Slow, deliberate cursor movement; pause 2s after each verdict appears
- [ ] TTS or owner-narrated audio; background music optional at -20dB

**Research anchors (for narration depth or judge Q&A):**
- Kenya's next general election is constitutionally due in **August 2027** (second Tuesday of August, every fifth year) — the misinformation surge of a campaign season is a foreseeable, dated need.
- Misinformation around Kenyan elections is well documented — e.g. Mozilla's 2022 report on hired influencers shaping online opinion during the last campaign (verify exact title/link before citing in the video).
- Voice notes and forwarded screenshots circulate beyond the reach of text-first, English-first fact-checking — VeriVoice's Sheng/Kiswahili support targets exactly that gap.
