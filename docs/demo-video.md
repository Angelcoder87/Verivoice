## What the video must show

1. The problem: information arrives as voice notes and forwarded screenshots in Sheng, Kiswahili, and English.
2. Three inputs, one loop: a Sheng voice note, a typed county-budget claim, and a screenshot of a fake tender notice.
3. Verdict cards appearing one by one: Disputed / Misleading / Unverified, each with sources, a confidence level, and what we do **not** know.
4. The EN↔SW language toggle mid-demo.
5. Next steps referencing real Kenyan institutions and hotlines.
6. The shareable summary card going back to the group chat.
